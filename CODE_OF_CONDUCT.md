# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We pledge to make participation in the LibreStaff community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing discussions on the project and its users
- Taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes
- Respecting privacy and avoiding disclosure of personal or company information

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, insults, discriminatory language, sexualized
content, trolling, sustained disruption, publishing private information, or other conduct that would
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other official
LibreStaff community spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report abusive content using GitHub's private reporting and moderation tools. When additional private
contact is needed, use the contact methods listed on the repository owner's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/moh92ahm). Do not publish sensitive personal information while
reporting an incident.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Reports
will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
